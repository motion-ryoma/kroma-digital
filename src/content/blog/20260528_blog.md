## Which Tasks Take the Most Time?

Many small business owners spend time every day on repetitive tasks such as:

- Recording sales and expenses
- Checking inventory
- Replying to customers
- Searching for information in notebooks or Messenger
- Calculating profit manually

Kroma Digital wants to build simple tools that reduce the time spent on these tasks.
