## We Are Looking for Business Owners and App Testers

Kroma Digital is researching several app ideas for small businesses in Cambodia.

We are considering tools for:

- Sales and expenses
- Receipts
- Bookings
- Social media orders
- Inventory and customer information

The details of each app have not been fully decided.

First, we want to listen to business owners and understand which features are genuinely necessary.

We are looking for:

- Business owners who can tell us about their daily work
- People who have a business problem they want to solve
- People interested in testing an early app for free

If you are interested, please send Kroma Digital a message through Messenger.
