Kroma Digital was started with a simple idea: useful software should begin with a real problem.

In Cambodia, many small businesses still manage important work through paper, spreadsheets, chat messages, or a mix of different tools. These methods can work, but they can also create repeated work, missed information, and unnecessary complexity.

We do not want to build software first and then search for people who might need it. Our approach is the opposite.

## We listen before we build

We start by asking how the work is handled today, what causes problems, what people already do to solve those problems, and how often the issue occurs.

Only after understanding the workflow do we decide whether an app or digital tool is actually useful.

## Small tools can still create real value

A product does not need dozens of features to be valuable. A focused app that saves time every day, reduces mistakes, or makes records easier to understand can be enough.

That is the type of product Kroma Digital aims to build: simple Android apps and digital tools designed around practical needs in Cambodia.

## We want to hear real problems

If there is a repetitive, confusing, or time-consuming part of your work, we want to hear about it.

You can contact Kroma Digital through our Facebook page and tell us how you manage the work today.
