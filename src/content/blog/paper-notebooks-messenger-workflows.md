Many small businesses manage important daily work with paper, notebooks, or Messenger. Common examples include:

- Customer and order records
- Appointments
- Payments and expenses
- Inventory
- Delivery information

These methods are familiar, flexible, and easy to start with. They may be completely adequate for some businesses. But as the number of customers and transactions grows, information can become scattered, difficult to search, or easy to forget.

## The problem is not paper itself

We do not assume that every paper record needs to become an app. A new tool is only useful when it solves a real problem without making the work more complicated.

Before building, we want to understand:

- Which tasks take the most time?
- Which information is difficult to find later?
- Where do mistakes or missed messages happen?
- Which current methods already work well?

## Tell us how you work today

What do you currently manage with paper, notebooks, or Messenger? Which task takes the most time or causes the most mistakes?
